Resolution.

This meeting of LIC Pensioners of Muzaffarpur Division held at Muzaffarpur on 18.11.2026 notes with deep concern and consternation that many of the pressing issues of the insurance pensioners remain unresolved till today despite the fact that these stand represented to the authorities concerned umpteen number of times. The inordinate delay in settling the issues has been causing untold sufferings, monetary and otherwise, to the pensioners apart from denying justice to them. The issues are as follows:

a)

Pension Updation: The most pressing of all the issues that confront the insurance pensioners is the matter of periodical revision of the basic pension following the wage revision of the in-service employees as is in vogue in the case of Central Government Employees. Pension Rules, 1995 of LIC make it abundantly clear that the rules were framed on the basis of Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules. But the improvements that came to Central Civil Service Pensioners as per the recommendations of the Central Pay Commission providing Pension Updation, are being denied unjustly to the insurance pensioners. Besides, such a pension updation scheme was also extended to the RBI pensioners later. But, despite repeated representations, insurance pensioners stand deprived of this benefit. Consequently the Basic Pension of the insurance pensioners continues to remain stagnant. This has created serious distortion, anomaly and discrimination. The management of LIC of India, unfortunately, continue to take the plea that the matter is sub-judice since it is under adjudication before the Supreme Court. But given a positive approach from the LIC management an out-of court settlement of this issue can be made possible.

This meeting strongly urges upon the LIC management to consider the matter with all the urgency, it deserves, and come out with a positive stance on this issue.

b)

Uniform DA neutralisation for pre-August, 1997 pensioners: This is another long pending issue crying for solution. As far back as in November, 2001 LIC Board approved and recommended to the Central Government for full neutralisation of DA for pre-August, 1997 pensioners. But, unfortunately, the matter still remain undecided. This meeting, therefore, urges upon LIC management to resolve the issue in line with the LIC Board resolution.

c)

Substantial increase in ex-gratia to pre-1986 retirees: This is another issue that stand represented to the management several times. This meeting urges upon the LIC management to consider an upward revision of ex-gratia to the above category of retirees, who are super seniors and whose number is very minimal, in line with at least whatever is provided by Union Bank of India, a public sector Bank, to such retirees.

d)

There are other important issues like Cash Medical Benefit for domiciliary treatment, Preventive health check up, Improvements in Mediclaim Scheme including increase in Cadre Sum Insured, Full subsidy in premium for cadre sum insured to all pensioners, Coverage of dental treatment, cost of hearing aids and other age related expenses, full reimbursement of cost of robotic surgery without restricting the benefit to certain procedures only, Withdrawal of GST on the premium of Group Mediclaim schemes, Consideration of apprentice period of Class II Development Officers to derive qualifying period of service for computing pension, Increase in basic pension when a pensioner reaches the age of 80 and above as is in vogue in the case of Central Government Employees etc. All these issues are waiting for solution. The delay in resolving these issues has made the pensioners extremely restive.

This meeting, therefore, urges upon the management to take steps to arrive at a satisfactory solution of all these issues at the earliest.

Adopted unanimously.
